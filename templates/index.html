Beta Angle

Beta angle defines the rotation of the member's local Y-Z axes about the local X-axis.

β = 0° means no rotation.

Positive beta follows the right-hand rule about the local X-axis.

Negative angles are allowed.

The beta angle is assigned independently of the section property.
